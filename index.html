Space Junkz is a progression-driven indie space shooter built from scratch with HTML5 Canvas, Three.js and vanilla JavaScript. Fight through handcrafted missions, destroy 3D bosses, recover WizzCoins and Salvage, unlock stronger weapons, hunt Rare Contacts, secure powerful caches and push deeper into hostile space.

Permanent Progression

Return to Home Base. Rebuild stronger.

The Ankh Chamber is the campaign progression hub. Store recovered resources, spend WizzCoins and Salvage at the Crafting Obelisk, and open secured combat caches for persistent rewards tied to your local profile.

Battle Scorpion and the Gemini Warship in the intro, survive the four Drax command ships, face Gemini Rift’s five-boss sequence, break the Shattered Armada and its escorts, then endure sustained pressure until the Void Frontier boss arrives.

Current Build Update

The Three.js expansion is live.

Space Junkz now integrates Three.js across its combat presentation, World Map and redesigned HomeBase, with over 40 new GLB models added during development. Mission 6 introduces forward-flight combat, while Mission 7 opens full 3D free-roam flight. New bosses, ship refits and an interactive hangar make this the game's biggest visual expansion yet.
