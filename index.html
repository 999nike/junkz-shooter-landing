Permanent Progression

Your hangar. Your fleet. Your next move.

The walkable 3D hangar is your home between missions. Store recovered resources, spend WizzCoins and Salvage at the Crafting Obelisk, and open secured combat caches for persistent rewards tied to your local profile.

InventoryKeep WizzCoins, Salvage, cache totals and persistent profile rewards.
Crafting ObeliskLaser Core, Rapid Fire, Twin Shot and Plasma Lance are live permanent weapon upgrades.
Cache VaultOpen Rare, Legendary and Mythic caches for their different resource and upgrade rewards.
Ship ShowroomBrowse hulls including Space Bomber, Harbinger and Titan Prime, then fit armour, shields, weapons and a power-up stabiliser.

Earn • Upgrade • Persist

Turn every mission result into progression.

Resources, profile XP, arcade score and mission records persist between runs. Temporary firepower builds during combat while Crafting Obelisk upgrades and cache rewards permanently strengthen your ship.

🪙 WizzCoins

Primary currency used alongside Salvage for permanent upgrades.

♻️ Salvage

Recovered from missions and used for HomeBase upgrades.

📈 XP and Score

Earn pilot levels from lifetime XP. Track your next level, mission records and lifetime statistics; level-ups appear in gold during play.
