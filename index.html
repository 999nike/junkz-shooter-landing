Legendary Cache Protocol

A full boss can drop one cache. Contact and the pickup magnet cannot collect it: player weapons must break its own health pool, triggering a boss-scale explosion and a single persistent acquisition. Open it at HomeBase to receive one weighted permanent ship-stat increase.
