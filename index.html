Features

Core Gameplay Loop

Fight through enemy waves, collect Coins and Salvage, defeat the boss, return to Home Base and craft permanent upgrades for the next mission.

Multi-Phase Boss Battles Boss families

Learn unique attack patterns, survive giant projectile barrages and defeat expanding boss families with distinct mechanics.

Weapons & Ship Evolution

Transform your starter ship through permanent upgrades including Twin Shot, Rapid Fire, laser technology, shield systems and combat sidekicks.

Permanent Progression

Return to Home Base. Rebuild stronger.

Every mission feeds back into your ship. Bank Coins, recover Salvage and use the Crafting Obelisk to unlock permanent combat upgrades before taking on harder worlds.
