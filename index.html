Fight through handcrafted missions, destroy multi-stage bosses, recover WizzCoins and Salvage, unlock stronger weapons, hunt Rare Contacts, secure powerful caches and push deeper into hostile space.

Permanent Progression

Return to Home Base. Rebuild stronger.

The Ankh Chamber is the campaign progression hub. Store recovered resources, spend WizzCoins and Salvage at the Crafting Obelisk, and open secured combat caches for persistent rewards tied to your local profile.
